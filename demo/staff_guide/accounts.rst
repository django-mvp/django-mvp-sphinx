Accounts
========

``seed_demo`` creates three accounts, all with the password ``password``.

* ``regular.user@example.com`` is signed in but not staff. The staff guide
  answers this account with the forbidden page and the sidebar has no entry
  for it.
* ``staff.user@example.com`` is staff and reads this page.
* ``super.user@example.com`` is a superuser and is also staff.
