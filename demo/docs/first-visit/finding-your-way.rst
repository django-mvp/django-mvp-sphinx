Finding your way
================

Every page of the demo has the same frame, so once you know where things are
you know where they are everywhere.

The sidebar
-----------

The sidebar on the left lists the pages of the site: the overview, this guide and,
for staff, the staff guide. Inside this guide it also lists every page, in the
order they are meant to be read. Captions such as *Using the site* and *Reference*
group them. A page with pages of its own, like :doc:`index`, opens as a group: its
first entry is the page itself, and the pages below it follow.

On a narrow screen the sidebar folds away. A button in the top bar opens it again.

Breadcrumbs
-----------

Above each page's title a trail shows where you are. On a page inside a group it
starts at the name of the guide and passes through each page above this one. Follow
any part of the trail to go up a level.

The search box
--------------

Every page of the guide has a search box at the top of its text. Type a word and
press Enter: the results list the pages that hold it, each with a short passage
around the word. Searching covers the guide only, not the other pages of the site.

On this page
------------

A long page lists its own headings in a panel beside the text, titled *On this
page*, once the window is wide enough to fit it. Follow a heading to jump to it.
Sub-headings sit under their heading.

Previous and next
-----------------

At the foot of each page, links lead to the page before it and the page after it,
following the order of the sidebar. The first page has no previous link and the last
page has no next link.

Light and dark
--------------

The theme control at the bottom of the sidebar switches the look of the whole site.
The demo offers four themes: light, dark, corporate and dracula. Your choice is
remembered in the browser. Try the same page in two themes: the guide reads the
same in each.
