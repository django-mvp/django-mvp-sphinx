Signing in
==========

You do not need an account to read this guide, and you do not need one to look
at the demo's other pages. You sign in to try the parts of the site that depend
on who you are.

The demo comes with three accounts. All three use the password ``password``.

#. Choose **Log in** at the bottom of the sidebar. It is the button above the
   theme control.
#. Type the email address of one of the accounts, and the password.
#. Submit the form. You land on the overview page, and the sidebar now shows
   your name where the button was.

To sign out, open the menu your name opens at the bottom of the sidebar and
choose **Log out**.

Which account to use
--------------------

:doc:`/reference/accounts` has every address, with what each account can open.
In short, use ``regular.user@example.com`` to see the site as an ordinary
signed-in visitor, and ``staff.user@example.com`` to open the :doc:`staff guide
</staff-guide>`.

.. note::

   The accounts exist only on the demo. They are made by a command that refuses
   to run when the site is not in debug mode, so the password is not something
   to reuse anywhere else.

.. tip::

   Signed in and looking for a guide page? Nothing changes for the pages of this
   guide. Only the sidebar's staff entry appears or disappears.
