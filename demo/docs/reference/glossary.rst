Glossary
========

The words this guide uses for the parts of the site and the accounts you sign in
with.

.. glossary::

   sidebar
      The column at the left of every page. It lists the pages of the site and,
      inside this guide, the pages of the guide. See :doc:`/first-visit/finding-your-way`.

   regular account
      An account that can sign in but is not staff. ``regular.user@example.com``
      is one.

   staff account
      An account that can open the staff guide. ``staff.user@example.com`` is one.

   superuser account
      An account with every permission the site has. It is also staff, so it reads
      the staff guide too. ``super.user@example.com`` is one.

   front page
      The page a guide opens on. It is the first entry of the sidebar's list for
      that guide.

See :doc:`accounts` for the three accounts side by side.
