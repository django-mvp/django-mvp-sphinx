Who sees what when they sign in, and why the sidebar differs between one visitor and the next
==============================================================================================

The sidebar is not the same for everyone who opens the site. Most of it is: the
overview and this guide are open to all. One entry depends on who is asking.

The entry that comes and goes
-----------------------------

The staff guide's entry is shown to staff accounts and hidden from everyone else.
An entry that is hidden is not greyed out or crossed through: it is simply not there,
so a visitor cannot tell it exists from the sidebar alone.

If you know the address, you can still try it. What you get back is told in
:doc:`/staff-guide`, and what each of the three accounts gets is tabled in
:doc:`accounts`.

What stays the same
-------------------

The pages of this guide read the same for every visitor, signed in or not. The
breadcrumbs, the search box, the previous and next links and the *On this page*
panel do not depend on who you are.
